FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need to install anything on my server?

No. Your staff use a web panel, and the only download is the scanner the player runs on their own PC.

Who can create scan links?

Only signed-in staff. Staff log in with Discord, and the Staff plan button then appears at the top right of the site.

Can a cheater clean their PC first?

They can try, but cleaning leaves its own evidence. Nex Core reports the cleanup as a finding.

Does it ban players automatically?

No. A person on your team reads the report and sets the result.

What does a scan upload?

Only what a review needs: flagged file names, signs of cleanup, accounts on the PC, and basic device info.
